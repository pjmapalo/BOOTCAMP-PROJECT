
# GLOBAL SERVICE AGREEMENTS PERFORMANCE REVIEW # 
Our company does not have a clear way to see which service agreements make money and which ones create financial risk. This lack of visibility makes it harder for teams to make informed decisions about pricing, renewals, and where to focus resources. 

## PROBLEM STATEMENT
The total revenue is 10% below target and % profitability margin should be atleast 20%

## METHODOLOGY
   MICROSOFT EXCEL
   Clean data
   POWER BI
   Dax Measure (Total Revenue, Cost and Profit, Expiring Agreements Coun, Filter Top N)

## INSIGHTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

<img width="600" height="131" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9d1f497c-4c35-4a3e-81d1-b2d060585541" />


The portfolio generates ~8.72bn in revenue against 7.47bn in cost, delivering ~1.25bn profit and a 14.35% profit margin across 236 service agreements


<img width="600" height="150" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0f0061d6-1897-4b88-9f28-f1f4ed795b9e" />


Performance is highly concentrated: a few countries (especially Canada, Australia, South Africa, Mexico, India, Brazil, Chile and Peru) drive the majority of revenue. For example, Canada alone contributes ~1.67bn, the single largest country share in the file


<img width="600" height="150" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e82292ff-8cda-4662-b490-429a1e65f51e" />

There is a long tail of smaller or loss‑making agreements where costs are equal to or higher than revenue (negative profit margin). These are mainly scattered across Kazakhstan, Zambia, some Latin American and African contracts, and selected NASA agreements.


<img width="600" height="200" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e9b6a62-5474-4fe0-9790-89520fe6b5ec" />


A number of contracts are “Expired/Expiring Soon”, especially in Kazakhstan, Zambia, Peru, Mexico, South Africa and Chile, which represents both risk (lost revenue) and opportunity (repricing / margin improvement)




Recommended actions: 
1. Fix or exit loss‑making contracts
2. Protect and grow our “star” accounts
3. Strengthen cost recovery & renewal discipline

If we systematically correct loss‑making agreements and tighten renewals on expiring contracts, we can lift the portfolio margin from 14.35% towards our target while protecting revenue in our key countries
